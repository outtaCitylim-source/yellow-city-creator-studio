# Yellow City Creator Studio - Ecosystem Alignment

## Core rule
Every part of the project must feed the same ecosystem instead of becoming separate disconnected tools.

Yellow City Creator Studio is the operating system. AmarilloThreads is the public doorway. Yellow City Custom Tees is the production and customer relationship hub. Dillon Richards is the creator and systems builder. Ashton Hammer is the owner and shop lead. Outta City Limits remains Dillon's broader entrepreneurial brand and proof of business-building capability, but it should not compete with Yellow City Custom Tees inside this app.

## Brand roles

### Yellow City Custom Tees
Role: The real Amarillo print shop and production center.

Purpose:
- Custom apparel production
- DTF, DTG, embroidery, vinyl, banners, and event merch
- Walk-in customer service
- Local business accounts
- Repeat customer relationships

Positioning:
Yellow City Custom Tees should be shown as the shop that makes the work real.

### AmarilloThreads.com
Role: The public-facing web doorway.

Purpose:
- Public website
- Customer trust
- SEO and local discovery
- Quote-entry traffic
- QR campaign destination
- Social media and print marketing destination

Positioning:
AmarilloThreads should be the clean public URL customers remember.

### Yellow City Creator Studio
Role: The digital operating system.

Purpose:
- Quote intake
- Lead queue
- Order workflow
- Production board
- Customer CRM
- Inventory ops
- Event portal
- Team store framework
- Owner dashboard
- Proof and analytics

Positioning:
Creator Studio is not just a website. It is the shop command center.

### Yellow City Creator Bar
Role: The in-store customer experience.

Purpose:
- Touch blanks
- View colors and sizes
- Upload artwork
- Build a quick mockup
- Approve proof direction
- Place order before leaving

Positioning:
Creator Bar is the physical customer-facing version of Creator Studio.

### Outta City Limits
Role: Dillon's wider entrepreneurial ecosystem and credibility layer.

Purpose:
- Shows Dillon can build systems, brands, sales workflows, and digital operations
- Supports future expansion, resale, collectibles, commerce, marketing, and cross-project experience
- Should stay secondary inside YCCT-facing materials unless specifically relevant

Positioning:
Outta City Limits should support Dillon's credibility without confusing customers about who owns Yellow City Custom Tees.

## Ecosystem flow

```text
Customer sees QR, social post, flyer, Google listing, or AmarilloThreads.com
        ↓
Customer enters Quote Intake or visits the shop
        ↓
Creator Studio captures the lead
        ↓
Staff reviews quote and proof needs
        ↓
Order enters production board
        ↓
Inventory and deadline risks are visible
        ↓
Customer receives follow-up or pickup direction
        ↓
Customer history powers reorders, loyalty, team stores, and future campaigns
```

## Revenue loops

### Walk-in loop
Walk-in customer enters the shop, uses Creator Bar, sees blanks and options, submits or approves a quote, and leaves with a clear next step.

### Business account loop
Local Amarillo businesses submit branded apparel needs, become saved customers, and later reorder uniforms, polos, hats, and event merch.

### Event loop
Route 66, local school, graduation, Harley, and community events use QR intake so booth traffic becomes trackable leads instead of lost conversations.

### Team store loop
Teams, schools, clubs, and fundraisers get structured intake, size collection, package selection, and reorder history.

### Reorder loop
Past customers can reorder from saved history instead of starting from zero.

## What must coexist

The system should make these parts work together:

- Public marketing
- In-store experience
- Quote intake
- Customer CRM
- Order production
- Inventory tracking
- Event campaigns
- Team stores
- Reorders
- Owner analytics
- GitHub source control
- Vercel deployments
- Future payment and email services

## What must not happen

- Do not make AmarilloThreads, Yellow City Creator Studio, and Yellow City Custom Tees look like unrelated brands.
- Do not let Outta City Limits overpower the YCCT customer-facing experience.
- Do not present demo features as fully operational without proof.
- Do not merge production changes without CI, preview, and manual QA.
- Do not use Route 66 assets in a way that implies official affiliation unless permission is confirmed.

## Owner-facing message
This ecosystem gives Ashton a cleaner shop operation and gives Dillon a clear role building the systems, design workflows, customer intake, marketing, and technical infrastructure that let Yellow City Custom Tees grow.

The business value is not just software. The value is fewer lost orders, faster quoting, cleaner production, better customer follow-up, stronger event sales, and repeat revenue from customers who already trust the shop.
